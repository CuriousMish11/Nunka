Services

One method, four kinds of property

Every consultation starts from your plan and a compass reading taken at the centre of the property. The advice changes with what you are building, buying or living in.

Home

A zone-by-zone reading of your flat or house: entrance, kitchen, bedrooms, puja room and the centre.

  • Existing homes and new purchases
  • Bedroom and sleeping-direction advice

Office and shop

Placement of the owner's cabin, cash counter, reception and pantry, so the space supports the work done in it.

  • Seating and facing directions
  • Entrance and signage placement

Plot selection

Before you buy, we check the plot's shape, slope, road position and surroundings.

  • Square and rectangular plot checks
  • Road-facing (vīthī) assessment

Remedies without demolition

Corrections through placement, colour, lighting and use of space, ranked from simplest to most involved.

  • Furniture and storage shifts
  • Colour and element balancing
How a consultation works

From floor plan to follow-up

  1. Share your plan

    Send the floor plan or plot drawing, a few photos and the address.

  2. Direction reading

    A compass reading from the Brahmasthan fixes true orientation and the zones.

  3. Zone analysis

    Each room is checked against its zone, its element and its use.

  4. Remedy report

    A written report, with fixes ordered from easiest to largest.

  5. Follow-up

    A review call after changes, to answer questions and adjust.
